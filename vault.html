    Good to know

    • Files stay in your own Google Drives, in each account's SA Vault folder. Even without this website, you can open them in Google Drive.
    • Nothing is deleted straight away. Deleted files and the old copies left after a move go to the vault trash (Storage page), where you can restore them or free the space. Google empties its trash after 30 days.
    • Sign in to every connected Gmail account at least once every few months. Google deletes accounts unused for 2 years.
    • Always upload through the vault, so files count against the right drive.
    • Open the vault on the office laptop while it is connected to the server: the backup runs by itself.
